**Status:** Published

Real-world pavement management is subject to significant uncertainties — in pavement deterioration rates, material costs, traffic growth, and budget availability. Deterministic optimization models cannot adequately address these risks.

This project introduces a **chance-constrained programming** approach to network-level pavement M&R planning, ensuring that budget and performance constraints are satisfied with a specified probability even under uncertain conditions.

Key contributions include:

- Formulation of pavement M&R planning as a stochastic optimization problem with probabilistic constraints
- A tractable solution methodology that converts chance constraints into deterministic equivalents
- Case studies demonstrating improved reliability of long-term maintenance plans compared to deterministic approaches
