**Status:** Accepted at _Journal of Computing in Civil Engineering (ASCE JCCE)_

Background subtraction is a fundamental step in LiDAR-based traffic monitoring. However, existing methods struggle with environmental noise, sensor drift, and the sparse, non-uniform nature of roadside point clouds.

**FRGB3D** introduces a fast, reliability-weighted Gaussian mixture model tailored for roadside LiDAR point clouds. Key contributions include:

- A reliability weighting scheme that accounts for varying point density and sensor noise across the field of view
- A computationally efficient background modeling pipeline suitable for real-time traffic monitoring
- Demonstrated robustness under adverse weather and varying traffic conditions

The method provides a strong foundation for downstream tasks such as vehicle detection, tracking, and speed estimation in infrastructure-based sensing systems.
