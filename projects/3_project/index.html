MulTrack3D

Robust multi-point tracking for multiple roadside LiDARs via voting-based consensus

Paper status: Under review after major revision · Automation in Construction (2026)

Maintaining consistent object identities across frames is a core task in roadside LiDAR perception. Sparse observations and occlusion can interrupt vehicle trajectories and affect downstream traffic analysis.

MulTrack3D studies robust multi-point tracking for multiple roadside LiDARs through voting-based consensus. The framework connects observations over time to support continuous vehicle trajectories.

The work forms the tracking stage of the quality-aware multi-LiDAR research framework, linking object detection with trajectory-based traffic and safety analysis.
