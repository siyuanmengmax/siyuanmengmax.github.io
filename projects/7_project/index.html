Personalized VISTA

Vision-Language Safety Assistant for Trustworthy Autonomy

Status: In Preparation — Target: NeurIPS

As autonomous vehicles become increasingly capable, ensuring trustworthy and explainable decision-making in safety-critical scenarios remains an open challenge. Vision-language models (VLMs) offer a promising path toward human-aligned autonomy.

Personalized VISTA develops a vision-language safety assistant framework that provides personalized, context-aware safety guidance for autonomous systems.

Key aspects of this work include:

  • Integration of large vision-language models for scene understanding and risk assessment in driving contexts
  • A personalization mechanism that adapts safety recommendations to user preferences and driving styles
  • A trustworthiness framework that balances autonomy with human oversight in safety-critical situations

This project represents an intersection of my work in intelligent transportation systems and emerging foundation model capabilities, targeting trustworthy deployment of autonomous vehicles in complex real-world environments.
