FRGB3D

Fast Reliability-Weighted Gaussian Background Modeling for Roadside LiDAR Traffic Monitoring

Paper status: Published · Journal of Computing in Civil Engineering, 41(1), 04026129 (2027)

Background subtraction is a fundamental step in LiDAR-based traffic monitoring. Variations in measurement reliability caused by distance, surface reflectivity, and atmospheric conditions make background–foreground separation challenging.

FRGB3D introduces a fast, reliability-weighted Gaussian background model tailored for roadside LiDAR point clouds. Key contributions include:

  • A physics-based reliability metric that quantifies measurement quality using range and reflectivity
  • Reliability-weighted adaptive learning that adjusts background model learning rates based on point reliability
  • Adaptive foreground detection thresholds that incorporate measurement reliability and learned model uncertainty

Experiments using CARLA and real-world LiDAR data from Lowell, Massachusetts, evaluate performance across weather and traffic conditions. The real-world experiments achieve 19.1 frames per second without requiring labeled training data or GPU infrastructure, supporting practical roadside traffic monitoring.
