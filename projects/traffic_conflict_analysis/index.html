Quality-Aware Traffic Conflict Analysis

Spatial precision, tracking quality, and traffic conflict detectability in roadside LiDAR systems

Paper status: Submitted · Transportation Research Part C: Emerging Technologies (2026)

Traffic conflict analysis relies on the positions and trajectories recovered by roadside sensors. This research examines how sensing and tracking quality affect the interactions that can be detected and interpreted.

Two related studies address spatial precision enhancement for multi-LiDAR conflict detection and the relationship between tracking quality and conflict detectability, using ClusterTrack3D as the tracking method under study. Together, they connect roadside perception quality with traffic safety analysis.

Related publications

  • Siyuan Meng, Yu-Min Yang, Eleni Christofa, Michael Knodler, Chengbo Ai. Quality-Aware Vehicle Conflict Detection with Spatial Precision Enhancement for Roadside Multi-LiDAR Systems. Transportation Research Part C: Emerging Technologies (2026). Submitted.
  • Siyuan Meng, Chengbo Ai. Tracking Quality Determines Traffic Conflict Detectability in Roadside LiDAR Systems: Evidence from ClusterTrack3D. Journal of Computing in Civil Engineering (2026). Submitted.
