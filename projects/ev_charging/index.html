EV Charging and Queue Management

Station information, request timing, and queue-aware station choice for highway corridors and mixed fleets

Paper status: Submitted · Transportation Research Part D: Transport and Environment (2026)

This research studies how information and charging decisions affect queues in electric transportation systems.

  • Intercity highway corridors: investigate station information and request timing for reducing fast-charging queues.
  • Electric ride-hailing fleets: examine when queue-aware charging-station choice is beneficial in fleets with both autonomous and human-driven vehicles.

These studies form part of a broader research direction on routing and charging coordination under demand and behavioral uncertainty.

Related publications

  • Siyuan Meng, Chengbo Ai. Station Information and Request Timing for Reducing Fast-Charging Queues on Intercity Highway Corridors. Transportation Research Part D: Transport and Environment (2026). Submitted.
  • Siyuan Meng, Chengbo Ai. When Does Queue-Aware Charging-Station Choice Pay Off in Mixed Autonomous and Human-Driven Electric Ride-Hailing Fleets?. IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems (2026). Submitted.
