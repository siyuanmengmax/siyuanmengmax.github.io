Ph.D., UMass Amherst

I received my Ph.D. in Civil Engineering (Transportation Engineering) from the University of Massachusetts Amherst in September 2026, advised by Dr. Chengbo Ai.

My dissertation, Quality-Aware Multi-LiDAR Framework for Traffic Perception and Safety Analysis, developed a reliability-aware pipeline spanning background modeling, object detection, tracking, and traffic safety analysis.

Before UMass Amherst, I earned my M.S. and B.S. degrees at Chang’an University, where my master’s research was advised by Dr. Qiang Bai. My earlier work focused on pavement maintenance optimization and deep learning-based airport curbside vehicle detection.
