I received my Ph.D. in Civil Engineering (Transportation Engineering) from the [University of Massachusetts Amherst](https://www.umass.edu/) in September 2026 (GPA: 3.97/4.0), advised by [Dr. Chengbo Ai](https://aichengbo.com/). My Ph.D. dissertation, **“Quality-Aware Multi-LiDAR Framework for Traffic Perception and Safety Analysis,”** developed a reliability-aware framework for roadside multi-LiDAR perception and transportation safety analysis.

My research centers on **Infrastructure-Centric World Models (I-WM)** — a framework that leverages the unique _temporal depth_ of fixed roadside sensors to build reliable, evolving representations of traffic environments. This vision connects my doctoral work on quality-aware multi-LiDAR perception with emerging directions in V2X cooperative perception, spatial intelligence, uncertainty-aware sensing, and world modeling. See: [arXiv:2604.17651](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17651).

My research spans three interconnected directions:

- **Intelligent Transportation Perception:** I develop infrastructure-based sensing and perception systems using roadside LiDAR networks, including background modeling (FRGB3D), unsupervised 3D object detection (MulDet3D), multi-object tracking (MulTrack3D), multi-sensor fusion, and infrastructure-centric world modeling.

- **Transportation Safety Analysis:** I investigate quality-aware traffic conflict detection and safety assessment using trajectory data generated by roadside sensing systems, with particular interest in propagating sensing reliability and uncertainty into downstream safety metrics.

- **Transportation Systems Optimization:** I develop multi-objective optimization frameworks for roadway asset management, EV charging infrastructure planning, and other sustainable and intelligent transportation applications.

My doctoral research established a complete quality-aware multi-LiDAR perception pipeline, spanning background modeling, object detection, tracking, and traffic safety analysis. FRGB3D has been accepted by the _ASCE Journal of Computing in Civil Engineering_, MulDet3D by the _ASCE Journal of Transportation Engineering, Part A: Systems_, and my road asset management research by _Transportation Research Record_. Additional work on multi-object tracking and roadside LiDAR-based safety analysis is currently under review.

Prior to UMass Amherst, I received my M.S. (GPA: 3.74/4.0) and B.S. (GPA: 3.76/4.0) degrees from [Chang'an University](https://en.chd.edu.cn/), China, where I was advised by [Dr. Qiang Bai](https://js.chd.edu.cn/ysgcxy/bq/list.htm). My earlier research focused on pavement maintenance optimization and deep learning-based airport curbside vehicle detection, resulting in a granted patent and software copyright.
